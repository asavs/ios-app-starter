# Spoken cues

Checked 2026-10-27. A: Apple; recommendation: put injectable speech output in the proposed cue module for timers, guided exercises and navigation. `AVSpeechSynthesizer` can speak the hold name and duration, with language-appropriate voice and rate. Apple recommends accessibility announcements for infrequent screen-reader-only events, rather than recreating VoiceOver; its speech session describes matching an utterance to an active assistive technology and audio-session choices ([WWDC20, A](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10022/)). These settings do not guarantee no overlap. Choose an explicit policy when VoiceOver is active: route essential phase changes through accessibility announcements, avoid duplicate synthesizer speech, and verify interruption and priority behavior on a real device. Respect the user's voice toggle; test localized names, voice availability, rates, music mixing and interruptions. Speech APIs support iOS 17. Background speech is subject to [background execution limits](background-audio.md).

Apple explains that `usesApplicationAudioSession = false` delegates speech session management to the system and may duck and mix audio from the app; this is not evidence that other apps' music will never duck. Using the shared session instead makes the app responsible for session policy and interruptions ([WWDC20, A](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10022/)). Check the actual behavior with music and VoiceOver together, and avoid stale queued utterances after pause or a missed phase.

## Module contract and reading

Workout instructions, mindfulness prompts and navigation directions can share a `SpeechOutput.speak(text:locale:priority:)` interface plus `cancel(sessionID:)`. Keep an injected synthesizer and fake; the cue coordinator decides which events are stale and whether VoiceOver announcements take precedence. Never promise automatic mixing or speech while suspended. Provide localized voice selection and default rate, avoid a growing queue when phases change quickly, and test speech interruption, VoiceOver, Bluetooth and a screen-locked device. Demo a spoken cue and a VoiceOver-safe announcement with a visible preference. Theme styles the demo; Settings stores the toggle, not the synthesizer.

Apple's [speech API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avspeechsynthesizer) and [WWDC20 session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10022/) document voices, assistive technology and session ownership (A). [Audio session](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avaudiosession) and [interruption handling](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/handling-audio-interruptions) describe shared-resource behavior (A). [WorkoutTimer](https://github.com/wollodev/WorkoutTimer) includes spoken countdowns alongside an engine (MIT, B); its README does not establish VoiceOver coordination. [VipassanaTimerApple](https://github.com/ajrnair/VipassanaTimerApple) describes guided speech alongside audio (MIT code, separate audio terms, B); verify its implementation and review state before reuse.
