# Spoken cues

Checked 2026-10-27. A: Apple; recommendation: app-specific announcements with an injectable cue interface, not a general module yet. `AVSpeechSynthesizer` can speak the hold name and duration, with language-appropriate voice and rate. Apple recommends accessibility announcements for infrequent screen-reader-only events, rather than recreating VoiceOver; its speech session describes matching an utterance to an active assistive technology and audio-session choices ([WWDC20, A](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10022/)). These settings do not guarantee no overlap. Choose an explicit policy when VoiceOver is active: route essential phase changes through accessibility announcements, avoid duplicate synthesizer speech, and verify interruption and priority behavior on a real device. Respect the user's voice toggle; test localized names, voice availability, rates, music mixing and interruptions. Speech APIs support iOS 17. Background speech is subject to [background execution limits](background-audio.md).

Apple explains that `usesApplicationAudioSession = false` delegates speech session management to the system and may duck and mix audio from the app; this is not evidence that other apps? music will never duck. Using the shared session instead makes the app responsible for session policy and interruptions ([WWDC20, A](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10022/)). Check the actual behavior with music and VoiceOver together, and avoid stale queued utterances after pause or a missed phase.
