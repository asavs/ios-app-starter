## 2026-10-26 — Appearance picker labels stack when the scheme switches mid-slide

- **Tools:** Xcode 27.0 on the Mac, iPhone 17 / iOS 27.0 simulator, the owner's iPhone 14 Pro on iOS 26.3. Task `ios-s5.13`.
- **Symptom:** Tapping Light or Dark in Settings made the segmented control's labels refract into stacked copies, three rows of "Dark", while the glass selection slid. Seen on the phone; reproduced by recording the simulator during `testSettingsScreen` and reading frames around the tap.
- **Cause (confirmed by the fix):** The picker wrote `settings.appearance` immediately, and `storedAppearance()` switched the window's color scheme while the iOS 26 glass selection was still animating, so its magnified label copies redrew in the new scheme out of place.
- **Fix:** `SettingsView` binds the picker to local state and writes the stored appearance 350 ms later, after the selection settles. A new tap restarts the wait, so only the last choice applies; a change from elsewhere updates the picker. Recorded frames now show the selection settle in light, then a clean switch to dark. The glass's own edge refraction during the slide remains; it is the system effect.
- **Verified:** Settings module tests (10 passed, 3 documented unsigned-host Keychain skips) and the app suite 7/7 on the iOS 27.0 simulator.
- **Delay sweep:** A temporary build read the delay from a launch argument; a throwaway UI test switched Light/Dark six times per run while the simulator was recorded, and a frame comparison measured how far the control strayed from its settled look after each switch (delay 0: 0.004-0.009, visibly stacked; 400 ms: 0.0006-0.0017). Stacked copies appeared at 0-100 ms; at 150 ms the switch still landed mid-slide, at 200-250 ms during the selection's settle, and from 300 ms the selection was settled first, leaving only the label's own short fade (0.0018-0.0024). The delay is 350 ms: the 300 ms threshold plus 50 ms for a busy device.
- **Remaining:** Measured on the iOS 27.0 simulator; the owner checked 400 ms on iOS 26.3. A future OS with a longer animation could bring the glitch back. Re-record frames after an Xcode upgrade.

## 2026-10-21 — Xcode 27.2 rejects XcodeGen's folder reference beside the local module package

The modules still show under Package Dependencies. `excludeFromProject: true` is not a fix: it drops the package reference too.
- **Verification:** Regenerated with `project.yml` and both examples: no folder reference is left. Locally on Xcode 26.6 the project resolves and links all four products and builds (with the icon fix from PR #83 applied locally); 3 of 5 UI tests passed there, and the 2 failures (multiple choice, Settings secret) are in app behaviour, not package resolution.
